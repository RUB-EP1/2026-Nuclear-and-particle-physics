## Preservation and reuse

Embedded images are extracted PDF image objects, including repeated logos/backgrounds and component images. They are not necessarily complete figures or original-resolution upstream assets. Full-slide previews preserve vector content and text omitted by image extraction. Do not infer experimental data points from pixels without a separate documented digitization. All third-party material retains its original rights; repository MIT licensing does not relicense it. This archive is tracked for internal preparation, outside the website render and resource lists.
