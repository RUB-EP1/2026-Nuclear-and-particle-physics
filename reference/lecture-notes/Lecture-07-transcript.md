```text
Calorimetry: Overview

• Basic mechanism for calorimetry in particle physics:
• formation of electromagnetic
• or hadronic showers.
• The energy is converted into ionisation or excitation of the matter.
• Calorimetry is a “destructive” method. —
  The energy and the particle get absorbed!

• Detector response ∝E
• Calorimetry works both for charged (e± and hadrons) and neutral particles (n,γ) !
```
