Fermi’s golden rule: from levels to a rate

One initial state coupled by a constant Tfi to a ladder of final levels with spacing d (density of states ρ = 1/d) · first-order perturbation theory · ħ = 1

Transition probability to each final level

sticks: Pf(t) = |Tfi|² sin²(ΔE t/2)/(ΔE/2)² at the allowed energies · curve: the window, scaled to its peak

Total probability to leave the initial state

solid: Σf Pf(t) · dashed: Γt with Γ = 2π|Tfi|² ρ

Let time run: the window narrows (energy conservation emerges) and the total grows linearly. Then make the levels sparser (large d) and watch the straight line fail.
