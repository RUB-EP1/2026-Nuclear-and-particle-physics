# Lecture 5: Interaction of Particles with Matter

Editable Quarto reconstruction of the new-material portion of last year's Lecture 5.
Slides include a board recap prompt, the new-material survey, a full-slide
interactive TPC widget, a full-slide Cherenkov widget, an Explore LHCb homework
slide, and backup material. The many-track ALICE image and the ALICE TPC video
are in backup.

The new material opens with an overview (particle signatures by detector layer,
and a process-by-particle map) and then runs through six numbered sections, one per
type of interaction.

Reserve 40 minutes for the board recap. The new material is a survey for roughly
50 minutes: ionization and TPC identification; fluctuations; radiation and
scattering; photon interactions; Cherenkov light. Skip the standalone Cherenkov
threshold exercise if discussion runs long. Presenter notes contain explanations,
answers and limitations. The recap is a prompt, not a developed 40-minute slide sequence.

Build with `quarto render slides/lectures/Lecture_5/Lecture_5.qmd` from the repo root.
Open the generated `Lecture_5.html`. All teaching images are local, so the slide
content works offline, including the TPC and Cherenkov widgets and the local
75-second TPC animation (backup); only the Explore LHCb links need a connection. Click the video controls to play; it preserves the original 4:3 aspect
ratio on the full slide. The Cherenkov widget has an Explanation button for its
complete derivation. Press F outside the widget to make the deck fullscreen.

See `SOURCES.md` for the old-to-new content map, source URLs and image licenses.
The requested `tmp/` folder contains per-page PDFs, page renders, text, embedded
images, downloaded source figures and visual QA. It remains ignored by Git.
