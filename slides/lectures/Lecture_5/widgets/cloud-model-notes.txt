Ambient cloud chamber: model scope

Transport: Geant4 11.3.2, FTFP_BERT through Geant4.jl 0.2.3 in the saved
2025 G4Examples environment. Box 200 x 200 x 50 mm, G4_AIR at its standard
material conditions. No magnetic field. 0.1 mm production range cut.
Geant4 energy-depositing charged-particle step segments are recorded.

Sources are deliberately simplified, not a calibrated room-background model:
* Cosmic muons: representative 4 GeV kinetic energy; mu+/mu- mixture 56/44.
  Horizontal-lid incidence with I(theta) proportional to cos^2(theta),
  including projected-area weighting. Uniform points on lid; no side-entry
  flux or building/altitude dependence. Normalization 1/cm2/min = 6.67/s.
* Rn-222: 5.49 MeV alpha, isotropic, uniform in chamber volume, with assumed
  100 Bq/m3 concentration. 2 litre full volume -> 0.2 decays/s. No daughter
  chain equilibrium, plate-out, or recoil modelling; excludes decays outside.
* Electrons: isotropic, uniform volume, 50 keV to 1 MeV uniform kinetic energy.
  Pedagogical surrogate for beta and gamma-induced electron tracks, NOT a
  physical beta spectrum or gamma-conversion calculation. Assumed 0.3/s.
* Independent Poisson arrivals. Playback uses a finite 50-event bank per
  source with square-symmetry rotations. Not fresh Geant4 events each time.

Visibility: bottom 10 mm selected by clipping, optionally all 50 mm (idealized).
Pure-air transport approximates the air/alcohol mixture. No chamber walls,
thermal gradient, supersaturation, diffusion, optical efficiency or true
nucleation calculation. Droplet positions/width/fading are illustrative;
particle traversal is effectively instantaneous, droplets then persist.
Inspection mode selects a long visible example (selection-biased, not rate data).
Speed control accelerates event arrival time, not optical trail persistence.
Neutral gamma rays do not directly make ionization trails; the electron
surrogate is labelled accordingly. No artificial magnetic spirals.

Display export: adjacent alpha step segments are coalesced in groups of up to four to keep the embedded bank compact. The Julia script regenerates full-resolution raw cloud-events.json; the HTML contains the reduced display bank.

Presentation view: orthonormal camera rotation and a single common x/y/z scale preserve physical proportions (20:20:5 cm). Blue slab marks the visible bottom layer. Dashed paths are teaching overlays, not visible condensation. Labels use each Geant4 track particle name/ID, including secondaries. Changing layer thickness retains the same event and orientation.

Realism review: 2026-10-30. Real-time playback is the default; 10x playback multiplies arrivals per wall-clock second while keeping optical persistence fixed, so it intentionally appears denser. Dashed full paths default off. Physical lid flux remains 400/60 muons/s, not a calibrated observed-track rate. All intersecting depositing segments are rendered without real optical detection inefficiency: short vertical muon traces can be much less noticeable in a real chamber. A single false-colour legend replaces track labels; real droplets are white.
