# Lecture 10: The Dirac Equation

KT1 2026/27. Companion slides for a blackboard lecture that follows Thomson, *Modern Particle Physics*, Chapter 4 (Dirac–Pauli representation, metric (+,−,−,−), normalization to 2E particles per unit volume). 58 slides: 49 numbered main slides and 9 unnumbered backup slides. Speaker notes (`S`) give timings and answers.

## Structure

- Recap: quantum mechanics (operators, ρ, j, continuity, eigenproblems, rank and degeneracy of `A x = 0`) and the "name a quantity" quiz (cards revealed one by one).
- 1 · Equations of motion: Lagrangian, Maxwell, which everyday waves solve Maxwell's equations, the table of field equations, why not Klein–Gordon.
- 2 · Dirac's idea: counting components (2 spins × 2 energy signs), γ matrices in the Dirac–Pauli representation, covariant form. α and β appear only in the backup.
- 3 · Reading the equation: anatomy, upper/lower indices, slash notation, explicit γ matrices, the four coupled equations, plane waves and `(p̸ − m)u = 0`.
- 4 · Solutions: at rest (rank 2), spectrum, moving particle, u₁…u₄, spinor explorer, current and the 2E normalization.
- 5 · Antiparticles: Dirac sea, Feynman–Stückelberg, zig-zag world line, v spinors, comparison tables.
- Summary, exit questions, backup (Dirac's α, β Hamiltonian, why four components, KG current, adjoint equation, γ toolkit, chiral representation, spin and g = 2, references).
