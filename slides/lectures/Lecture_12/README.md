# Lecture 12: Helicity and the Size of the Proton

KT1 2026/27, Tuesday 24 November 2026. Companion slides following Thomson, *Modern Particle Physics*, Chapters 6–7, same conventions as Lecture 11.

**Status: draft.** The opening sections were moved from Lecture 11, which did not reach them on 20 November. The rest is organised around one question: how big is the proton? Notation for E′, Q², τ, ε, G_E and G_M matches Sheets 6 and 7. Not yet linked from `index.qmd`. Plan in `slides/lectures.yml`.

## Structure

- Motivation (the proton radius puzzle).
- Recap: the end of Lecture 11 section 3 (slides copied from Lecture 11): contracting the two tensors, the recipe, Mandelstam variables, e⁻μ⁻ → e⁻μ⁻ and e⁺e⁻ → μ⁺μ⁻ at high energy, crossing, how many diagrams at lowest order (quiz), standard QED processes, QED cross-section explorer (widget).
- 1 · Spin and helicity: spin and helicity explorer (widget: spinor in the canonical and helicity basis), helicity as spin along the motion. Then a quiz on Lecture 11 (from its take-home messages and exit questions).
- 2 · Bridge: Rutherford scattering, two pictures of the atom, one hard collision (1/q⁴), Mott scattering (cos²θ/2 from helicity), replace the muon tensor by W^μν.
- 3 · Elastic electron–proton scattering: ladder of approximations, elastic kinematics (board), recoil in numbers, point-like Dirac proton, form factor as a Fourier transform (board), radius from the slope (board), same radius with different shapes, proton-radius explorer (widget), why small Q², G_E and G_M, the Rosenbluth formula.
- 4 · How big is the proton? Scattering versus spectroscopy, the day the proton shrank.
- Summary, exit questions, next time, backup: helicity structure of the QED vertex (no L–R mixing, allowed vertices, why 1 + cos²θ, no backward eμ scattering, pointer to γ⁵), helicity spinors for E ≫ m, references.
