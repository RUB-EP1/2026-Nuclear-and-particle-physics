Emittance: the area a beam occupies

Horizontal position x and angle x′ of every particle

Can any lens or drift make the cloud's area smaller? Watch ε while you try. Then accelerate.

—beam size σx
—angular spread σx′
—emittance ε (area / π)
—momentum

Drift: particles with an angle move sideways — the cloud shears.

Lens: kicks the angle in proportion to x — the cloud shears the other way.

Area is conserved (Liouville): you can trade size for angle, never both. A tiny spot needs a large angular spread — like focusing a flashlight.

Acceleration raises p but not px, so angles x′ = px/p shrink: ε ∝ 1/p.

Model: 600 particles, Gaussian beam; thin lenses (f = ±1.25 m) and drifts are linear maps that conserve area; acceleration divides x′ by the momentum ratio. Units mm and mrad, illustrative.
