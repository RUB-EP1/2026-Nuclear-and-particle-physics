Cockcroft–Walton: a voltage ladder

Each stage adds 2V0 · Vout → 2N·V0

Watch which diodes light up in each half cycle: charge is passed up the ladder like water in a bucket brigade.

No DC wire reaches the top: capacitors block DC. Charge gets up only through the diodes (lit, with moving dots), one rung per half cycle — a bucket brigade.

Left column (orange) passes the AC swing ±V0 upward, each node sitting 2V0 higher than the one below.

Right column (blue) stores the charge as DC: 0, 2V0, 4V0, … Without load Vout → 2N·V0; a beam current makes it sag and ripple.

Circuit model: equal capacitors, ideal diodes with a small series resistance, sinusoidal source of amplitude V₀, resistive load. Voltages shown in kV. Same circuit as the 1932 machine; real generators add stray capacitance and corona losses.
