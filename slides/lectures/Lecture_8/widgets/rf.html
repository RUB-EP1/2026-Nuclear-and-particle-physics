E (arrows) points along the beam and flips every half period. Cells are λ/2 = 11.5 cm, so a bunch at v ≈ c sees a forward field in every cell.

Where the field comes from: no coil — the cavity itself is the resonator. A klystron feeds RF power through a coupler; currents oscillate on the walls (a one-turn "coil") and make the circling B (green). Changing B induces E on axis (Faraday), changing E regenerates B: the energy sloshes between them, 90° apart.

Model: on-axis field Ez = E0(−1)kcos(ωt − π/2 + φ) in cell k, particle at v = c. Gains in units of E0·λ/2 per cell; the single-cell maximum 2/π is the transit-time factor. Real cavities have a smooth field profile; TESLA cavities run at about 20–35 MV/m.
